The problem

Password managers ask you to trust their cloud.

Reuse is the default

Without a manager, people reuse a handful of passwords. One leaked site opens every account that shares it.

Cloud vaults are targets

In 2022 attackers copied backups of LastPass customer vaults. From then on, each master password was the last line of defence.

Sign-ups and subscriptions

Most managers want an account first, and the most useful features often sit behind a monthly plan.

Source: LastPass security incident notice, December 2022.

How it works

Encrypted before it leaves your device.

01

Master password

Only you know it. Keyvane never stores it.

02

PBKDF2-SHA256 · 600,000 rounds

Turns the password into a key and makes guessing slow.

03

AES-256-GCM

Encrypts the vault with a fresh nonce on every save.

04

Ciphertext only

Chrome sync carries scrambled bytes. Google cannot read them.
