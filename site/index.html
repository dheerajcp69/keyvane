Encrypted before it leaves your device.

Keyvane has no server, so there's no company database of passwords to breach. Here's the whole path your data takes.

01 · You

Master password

Only you know it. Keyvane never stores it, not even encrypted.

02 · Key

Key derivation

PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds, random salt. This makes guessing attacks very slow.

03 · Vault

Encryption

AES-256-GCM with a fresh random nonce on every save, using your browser's built-in Web Crypto.

04 · Sync

Ciphertext only

Chrome sync carries scrambled bytes between your computers. Google can't read them.

The extension's security policy blocks every network connection, and the "save login" feature is off until you allow it. Read the privacy policy or check the code on GitHub.

Questions

Is Keyvane really free?

Yes. There are no plans, ads or paid features. Keyvane has no servers to pay for: your encrypted vault lives in your browser and syncs through your own Chrome sign-in.

Can anyone else read my passwords?

No. Your vault is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your master password with 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2. Keyvane never sends data anywhere, and Google only stores ciphertext when it syncs.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Nobody can recover it, including the developer. That is the price of real end-to-end encryption, so keep an encrypted backup from Settings and store your master password somewhere safe.

Can I move my passwords from Chrome, LastPass or Bitwarden?

Yes. Export a CSV from your browser or password manager and import it from Keyvane's Settings. Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Safari, Bitwarden, LastPass, 1Password, Dashlane and Proton Pass exports work.

Does Keyvane sync between computers?

Yes, through Chrome sync. Install Keyvane on each computer where you're signed in to Chrome and your encrypted vault follows you, with room for about 1,700 logins.

Does it work in Brave or Edge?

Keyvane installs from the Chrome Web Store in Brave and Edge. In those browsers the vault stays on that computer; use Settings, Export and Import to move it.
